Synthesizing quantum circuits

Qaptiva provides tools to automatically generate a quantum circuit based on a thrid party representation. Qaptiva appliance provides 4 tools to generate a quantum circuit, based on the initial representation:

  • CNOT circuit synthesis designed to generate CNOT circuits using, as input, linear boolean operators

  • CNOT + RZ circuit synthesis designed to generate CNOT + RZ circuits using, as input, phase polynomials

  • Variational Ansätze synthesis generating a variational circuit implementing \(e^{-i \gamma H}\) using, as input, an Hamiltonian \(H\)

  • Stabilizer state synthesis generating a circuit using, as input, a Clifford operator
